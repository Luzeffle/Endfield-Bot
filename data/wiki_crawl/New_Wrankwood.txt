The 
Free City of New Wrankwood
 is a city affiliated with the 
Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
 located within the 
Civilization Band
 on 
Talos-II
. Named after 
Wrankwood
 on 
Terra
, the city is known for its entertainment and film industry, hosting an annual Film Festival. The city is also home to the New Wrankwood Academy of Performing Arts and serves as a cultural hub known for its movie releases, fashion trends, and music albums.



Trivia


Like its counterpart 
Wrankwood
 on 
Terra
, New Wrankwood is based on 
Hollywood, Los Angeles
.
