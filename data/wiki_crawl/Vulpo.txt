Description


 
Gilberta, a notable Vulpo


Vulpos have the traits of 
foxes
 such as the 
true foxes
 (
Vulpes sp.
) and other fox-like canids (i.e., 
Lycalopex sp.
). Their vulpine traits can been noticed such as large, protruding ears and long, bushy tails unlike 
Lupos
 and 
Perros
. They are one of the three races separated from the Perros due to cultural and biological differences along with the Lupos and Reprobas.

On the outside, Vulpos are sometimes mistaken for Perros due to their almost identical physical appearances; hence, many Vulpos are already getting used to the mishaps. Nevertheless, they can be recognized through their medium-sized stature and their keen flexibility amongst other Talosians. In fact, in the ancient era before their divergence from the Perros, Vulpos were often described as the ”most agile of Perros.” Because of their traits, many Vulpos are depicted as outstanding combatants in various cultures. Many Vulpos often work as scouts, mercenaries, or Catastrophe Messengers since the dawn of human civilization.
