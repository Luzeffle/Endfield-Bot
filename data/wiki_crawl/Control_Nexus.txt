Description


Aerospace Materials are needed to construct and level up cabins aboard Dijiang. The player can get them from 
Recycling Stations
 they encounter during exploration.



Morale


The player must assign at least 1 Operator to a functional cabin to make it work. Every Operator assigned can raise the productivity of the cabin. The mood of Operators assigned to functional cabins will slowly decrease with time. An Operator with zero mood will automatically head to the Central Ring to rest, and will not any assignment effects during resting. At max mood, the operator will automatically return to the assigned functional cabin and resume working.



Trust


Raising Operators' trust unlocks more skills and profile data. Operators with higher trust also interact with the player differently aboard Dijiang. Interacting with Operators aboard Dijiang will give the player the option to talk to them or offer them gifts. Gifts will raise the Operators' trust, especially when the gifts are something they really like. More gifts can be obtained by talking to the Supply Chain Specialist aboard Dijiang and using the Protocol Exchange. The player will also earn trust slowly with time with Operators assigned to the Control Nexus or functional cabins. Even if they are not aboard Dijiang, these operators will continue to build up trust. The trust will be added to the operators once the player boards Dijiang.



Control Nexus


The Control Nexus is the management center of Dijiang. Use the 
Central Dashboard
 in the Control Nexus to view data of the entire ship and manage operator assignments of various cabins. Level up the Control Nexus to unlock more functional cabins aboard the ship.

Manufacturing Cabin


The 
Manufacturing Cabin
 can produce operator progression materials when given enough time. Select a manufacturing plan (MFG Plan) and the amount of items to make and operator(s) assigned to the 
Manufacturing Cabin
 will begin their work. 
NOTE: Manufacturing Cabins have a 
MFG Load
 and different items use up different amounts of MFG Load. Level up the Manufacturing Cabin to unlock more MFG Plans.

Protocol Exchange


Talk to the Supply Chain Specialist aboard Dijiang to exchange 
Protocol Exchange Certificates
 (PROXC) for various items. 
Buy out every item
 from the current phase to unlock the next phase of goods. Complete outpost orders to earn more 
Protocol Exchange Certificates
 (PROXC). 
NOTE: There is a limit for the amount of PROXC you can own. Any 
PROXC that exceeds this limit will expire on a regularly basis
. Always remember to spend your PROXC.
― Tutorial
