Azrila
 is a disciplined and reserved Sankta operator assigned to Endfield’s 
Specialist Tech Division
 under the codename 
Ember
. Before joining 
Endfield Industries
, she once served as an Oathkeeper Knight under the 
Order of Steel Oath
, fighting against the 
Aggeloi
 in the far north.



Background


As a young orphan, Azrila was taken in by the Order as a squire. She spent what remained of her childhood in the Order’s camps, training relentlessly in the frozen desolation of the Northern Frontier. For three consecutive years, she was able to reach the finals of the Steel Oath Annual Tourney—a feat few could achieve.
[
1
]


During a critical engagement on the Northern Front, the 
7th Cohort
 of the Order of Steel Oath unexpectedly lost contact, resulting in a major crisis for the Order. The loss of the 7th, responsible for guarding the rear supply line, threatened the stability of the entire front. Despite concerns over her inexperience, Azrila was tasked with leading a relief force to rescue the remnants of the 7th Cohort.
[
2
]


Leading her unit through the relentless northern rains, Azrila assessed the situation and determined that the 7th Cohort had likely already been annihilated. She redirected efforts toward responding to distress signals from the 5th Cohort, unaware that the Aggeloi had developed the ability to mimic human voices. This deception led Azrila and her unit into a devastating ambush. The ensuing battle resulted in catastrophic casualties, including the loss of Azrila's adjutant, Nilla.
[
note 1
]


Azrila barely survived. She was found unconscious after the battle and remained unresponsive for ten days. When she finally woke up, she could only recall the voices of her fallen comrades, haunted by their final calls for help. Guilt consumed her, and on the twentieth day of her recovery, she renounced war, declaring herself unworthy of the battlefield.
[
3
]
[
4
]
 She thus embarked upon a self-imposed exile and left the Order of Steel Oath.
[
5
]


Following her departure from the Order, Azrila accepted an invitation to join Endfield Industries. When asked to provide a codename, she hesitated before writing 
Ember
. Her time with the Order left her disciplined and unwavering in her duties. She executed her assignments with near-fanatical precision, as if seeking redemption for past failures. However, her rigid habits raised concerns among her peers—she often slept on the floor and left her dorm room barren, struggling to adapt to Endfield’s culture. When asked, she simply explained, 
"Beds in the south are too soft."
[
6
]


In an effort to adjust, Ember slowly began personalizing her room. Among her few belongings was a tattered leather-bound booklet titled 
Oaths
.
[
7
]
 Though she has left the battlefield behind, the weight of her past still lingers—one day, she may find herself returning to the north and confronting the very memories she has fought so hard to keep at bay.
