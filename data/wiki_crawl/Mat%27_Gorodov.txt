Mat' Gorodov
 (meaning "Mother of Cities") is the capital of the 
United Workers' Syndicates of Talos-II
 and is the political and manufacturing heart of the UWST on 
Talos-II
. Home to massive city plate construction yards and command towers, Mat' Gorodov serves as the primary production center for UWST forward bases and industrial infrastructure across Talos-II.



Quotes


Mat' Gorodov. I've seen this name countless times in UWST art films before my actual visit. She is the heart of the United Workers' Syndicates of Talos II, replete with massive command towers that scraped the skies and integrated city plate construction yards. She resembled the nomadic cities that I've seen in my memories — Solid and absolutely gargantuan. 
― 
Pogranichnik
