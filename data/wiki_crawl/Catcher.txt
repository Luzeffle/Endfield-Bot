Profile


"Need defensive cover? I shall give you my best."
Catcher serves on 
Operations Team Z7
 in Endfield, responsible for cover and logistical support. Backed by full military training, he draws on his extensive knowledge to guide his teammates through critical moments. Born into a military family within the 
Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
, he was raised with the love of devoted parents and older siblings. The pain and sickness caused by a childhood accident never brought him down. Instead, they shaped him into the steadfast warrior he is today.
Catcher may be the youngest on the team, but his maturity far surpasses that of his peers. He can work seamlessly with everyone, readily cover when a teammate struggles, and even prepare a camp meal tailored to everyone's needs. With him around, the team always feels safe and at ease.
"I have recently reviewed everyone's suggestions and improved my wildlands stew recipe... Feel free to give it a taste if you're curious, Endmin."
