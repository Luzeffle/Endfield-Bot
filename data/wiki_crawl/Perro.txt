Description


 
Akekuri, a notable Perro


Perros have the traits of 
dogs
 (
Canis familiaris
). Their canid traits can be easily noticed such as pricked, folded, or floppy ears, pointed or curled tails, and their keen sense of smell. In addition, their hair/fur is significantly more finer and softer compared to a 
Lupo
. Perros are known for demonstrating their emotions outwardly through their body parts such as wagging tail and folding ears. A notable culture practiced among the Perros are their large family and their loyalty to their families and friends. In a traditional Perro community, the families that make up the community are regarded as part of a larger family. Because of their family culture, Perros highly values peace and harmony between family and friends. Even when one faces difficulties, a Perro will always stay on their side.

Etymology


Their name is the Spanish word for "dog." It is worth noting that in Spanish, the word is sometimes used as a pejorative term.
