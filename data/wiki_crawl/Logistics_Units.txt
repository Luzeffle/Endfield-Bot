Logistics facilities are used to transport materials from one facility to another. They are used to create a seamless network across the complex.



Bridge


Bridges, as its name suggests, bridges two independent belts or pipes so they can cross each other without interruption.



Splitter


Splitters, as its name suggests, can split products from belts or pipes into all the connected output evenly up to 3 output.



Converger


Convergers, as its name suggests, can converge products from up to 3 belts or pipes into one.

Do keep in mind that both Transport Belt and Pipe both have a limited flow rate of 0.5 unit/s and 2 unit/s respectively, make sure to keep the total flow rate of the converged input does not exceed the limited flow rate to prevent clogging the production line.
