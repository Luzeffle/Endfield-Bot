Authority Level
 is a progression system in 
Arknights: Endfield
.



Description


Authority Levels are relevant to story progression, an example being a Level 43 requirement for access to 
Wuling
. They are also important for character progression, due to some of the 
Gear
 templates being locked behind Authority Levels.

The Authority Level can only be increased by obtaining 
 
Operational EXP
.
