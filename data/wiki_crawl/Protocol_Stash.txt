Item description
It took Endfield engineers nearly a decade to achieve tele-protocol of small items within limited range.


Usage


A storage facility with several item storage slots. Once connected to the power grid and given enough power, it can teleport items to the Depot. Hence, the stash is often placed at the end of production lines.
