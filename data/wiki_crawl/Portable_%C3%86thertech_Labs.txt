Weapons


Opus Stress
 - A contracted project developed by Portable Æthertech Labs. Cræft-adepts boldly furnished this weapon with a relicolith-lined barrel. The artslock firing mechanism was also fitted with a shard bearing cryptographic engravings.


Opus Etch Figure
 - A contracted product from Portable Æthertech Labs. Features metal components hand-engraved by cræft-adepts to create etch figures that match those of the tillite parts. The result is an Arts unit with unique Arts casting circuitry.
