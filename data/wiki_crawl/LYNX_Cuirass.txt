Item description
Designed by Rhodes Island and assembled by the AIC Factory. This equipment improves the wearer's combat capabilities.
The medics' preferred equipment. People usually refer to combatants wearing the LYNX Cuirass as "Walking Medkits". These lifesavers often stuff every pocket and holder of their armor with emergency medical supplies.
