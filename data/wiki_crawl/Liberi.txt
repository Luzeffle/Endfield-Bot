Description


 
Perlica, a notable Liberi


"Liberi" is an umbrella term referring to any 
Ancient
 and 
Elder
 race who have the traits of 
birds
 (
Aves sp.
), i.e., feathers growing on their hair and structures resembling tail feathers on their torsos, or feather-like features. Along with feathers, they also possess a good sense of hearing and eyesight. Generally speaking, Liberi are a medium-sized race with females being slightly shorter. Their physical fitness is relatively average at best, and they are considerably lighter in weight than other races. Some Liberi possess the traits of various bird-like mythological creatures in our world, while others resemble anthropomorphic avians.
