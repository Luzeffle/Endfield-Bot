The 
Order of Steel Oath
 is a faction on 
Talos-II
. Situated year-round on the northern frontier, the Order is a military organization primarily dedicated to defending human civilization against the 
Aggeloi
. It maintains a series of formidable fortresses and battlecarriers along the auroral north, serving as the first line of defense against any Aggeloid incursions. Their knights are known as Oathkeepers. 
Fort Forgefane
 is one such fortress belonging to the Order and is also an arms manufacturer and supplier that forges weaponry and armor for its knights.
[
1
]
 The Order is further divided into units called Banners and even smaller units called Cohorts.
[
2
]
 The assault banner often carry out Ankhor-busting missions, and is often nicknamed the "Nail" by fellow Oathkeepers. They would pierce through the defenses, hit the mark, survive the encirclement, and wait for the main hammering strike to silence the Ankhor permanently.

]
 The Shieldguard's roles include defensive formations, pushing battle lines, and providing frontline support.
