Raythean Industries
 (stylized as 
RAYTHEAN Industries
) is an arms manufacturer on 
Talos-II
. Originating from 
Columbia
, this international arms manufacturer and supplier has expanded its operations to Talos-II, establishing itself as one of the foremost arms suppliers on the moon.



Products


Weapons


Umbral Torch
 - A next-gen lightweight personal defense solution released by RAYTHEAN Industries. The sword has a special alloy blade and proved quite popular amongst certain "professionals".


Vortex of Talos
 - A classical product of the RAYTHEAN special customizable manufacturing process. This weapon features a diverse suite of special functions and customizable aesthetic components.


Clannibal
 - A special gun manufactured by RAYTHEAN Industries. This experimental weapon is extremely difficult to use. Only a limited number of prototypes were released to the arms market.


Rational Farewell
 - RAYTHEAN Industries developed this high energy pistol for frontiers use. Decent reliability in adverse environments make this a standard-issue weapon amongst wildland explorers.


Wild Wanderer
 - An Arts unit developed by RAYTHEAN Industries. This particular weapon is known for its minimalist and lightweight designs. Making it requires only half of the materials needed to produce a standard-issue Arts unit.
