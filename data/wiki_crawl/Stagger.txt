Stagger
 is a mechanic in 
Arknights: Endfield
.



Description


All enemies have a Stagger Meter, with Elite and Boss enemies generally have higher value. 
Operators
 can do Stagger via:



Final Strike


Skill, Combo or Ultimate; check Operators' skill for information


Arts Reactions


When Stagger Meter is broken, enemies will enter the 
Staggered
 state; When Staggered, enemies are immobile for a while, and the first Basic Attack from the currently controlled Operator becomes a Finisher. Before the recovery, Staggered enemies also take increased DMG. On recovery, enemies' Stagger Meter will be recovered to max.

Certain 
Weapons
 increase the Stagger dealt, while some alongside 
Gears
 provide personal bonus to Staggered enemies.
