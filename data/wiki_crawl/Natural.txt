Mineral Ore


Mineral ores
 are key resources found throughout Talos-II. You must attack and smash certain mineral formations into smaller pieces for gathering. Mineral ores have plenty of uses. Use them to support production and growth of your AIC Factory. They can also be used for crafting special facilities to help in your exploration.
― Tutorial


Found in various 
Mineral Beds
 in the overworld, Mineral Ores are crucial in AIC production as they can be used to craft 
AIC Products
 for either progression, for 
Outposts
 or for 
Usables
.

Type of Mineral Ores:



 
Originium Ore


 
Amethyst Ore


 
Ferrium Ore

Plant


Plants grew across the land of overworld that can be gathered directly, and is the main material for Usables through AIC or Crafting.

Type of Plants for production:



 
Buckflower


 
Citrome


 
Aketine


 
Sandleaf


 
Jincao


 
Yazhen


Some plants are unique in a sense that they can't be processed in AIC, but can still be used for Crafting:



 
Tartpepper


 
Reed Rye


 
Redjade Ginseng


 
Amber Rice


Some plants are variants of the plants that has a small chance to replace them as the product from gathering or planting, while also cannot be processed in AIC:



 
Firebuckle

Buckflower


 
Umbraline
 - variant of 
 
Citrome

 - variant of 
 
Jincao

Seed


The core of life of plants, seeds are only obtainable via putting the said Plants into a 
 
Seed-Picking Unit
.

Seeds from Plants that can be processed can be put into a 
 
Planting Unit
 to produce the corresponding Plant automatically, while those that cannot be processed can only be reproduced manually through planting. Variant Plants 
do not
 have Seeds to reproduce.



Planting


Use the 
Planting
 section of 
Crafting
 to craft Plots that can be used for planting crops. When you acquire a 
plant
, you will automatically unlock the crafting formula for the 
plot
 to grow that plant. When you have a Plot, you can place it in the specified area for planting. Planting has 4 stages: (1) Germinating, (2) Watering, (3) Growing, and (4) Harvesting. 
A fully grown plant can be harvested.
― Tutorial


With Seeds, 
Plots
 obtained from Crafting and a land suitable for farming in 
Originium Science Park
 or 
Wuling Outskirts
, the Plants can be grown and gathered naturally by manual planting.

To start, the Seeds need to be Germinated, afterwards the player need to water the Seeds for it to start growing. Growing takes a while (but the time runs offscreen), and afterwards, depending on the Seeds, the Plant will either be ready for harvesting, or need another section of watering.

Do note that Variant Plants can also be obtained this way.
