Overview


Little information is available on the Hannabit Circuit. It is described as a small nomadic organization that places great emphasis on training extremely skilled warriors who can hold their own.

1
]


Operator Arclight, real name Ekut, is a Scabhta of the Hannabit Circuit.

Trivia


During the second beta test, the Hannabit Circuit was originally called Fiannæ Circuit.

"Fiannæ" may have been inspired by the Gaelic word 
fiann
 (plural 
fianna
)
, which historically referred to bands of warrior-hunters and is central to Irish mythology. Additionally, the traditions of the Circuit may be based on the 
Fianna Cycle
 (or 
Fenian Cycle
), a body of early Irish literature focusing on the exploits of the mythical hero Finn or Fionn mac Cumhaill and his warrior band the 
Fianna
.
