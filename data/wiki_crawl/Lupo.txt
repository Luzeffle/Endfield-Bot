Description


 
Wulfgard, a notable Lupo


Lupos have the traits of 
wolves
 (
Canis lupus
). Their lupine traits can be noticed such as their pricked ears and bushy tails. They are one of the three races separated from the 
Perros
 due to cultural and biological differences along with the 
Vulpos
 and Reprobas. On the outside, they appear almost identical to a Perro, but they are also the easiest to be recognized through their unique culture, as well as having a generally coarse and hard hair in comparison.

Similar to the Perros, Lupos have a large family culture albeit being its opposite. Rather than treating their family members and friends kindly, a Lupo family treats them as disposable subordinates; antagonism between Lupo families is rather strong. They strongly value family identification such as dying of their tails, earpieces, and family symbols. Wherever one is banished from the family, they will be referred to as a "lone wolf," a derogatory term for a Lupine outcast. According to their legend and anthropological research, their strong yet crooked sense of family and their exclusivism are the consequences leads to Lupos learning to suppress their ancestral behaviors from the Perros.
