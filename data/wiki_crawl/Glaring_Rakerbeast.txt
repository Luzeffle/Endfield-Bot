Abilities








When compared to common rakerbeasts, this enemy will enter temporary stealth and charge up its skills at low HP. Interrupt it quickly to end its stealth and deal massive Stagger to it.


Enraged State: When enraged, the beast has increased ATK and uses its charge up skills more often. Applying Staggered to it ends the enraged state.
