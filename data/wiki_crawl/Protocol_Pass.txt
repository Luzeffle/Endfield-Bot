The 
Protocol Pass
 is the battle pass system in 
Arknights: Endfield
.

Protocol Pass allows players to earn 
 
Pass Experience
 and progress through various tiers to earn certain rewards, from Progression Materials, Profile decorations to even 
 
Oroberyl
 and 
 
Origeometry
. At the end of an Protocol Pass season, the progression will be reset and a new batch of rewards will be added.

Each Protocol Pass is divided into three tiers, with each tiers provide better rewards than the last:



Basic Supply are unlocked by default.


Originium Supply are unlocked with 
29 
.


Protocol Customized are unlocked with in-game purchase.
