Abilities








This enemy will charge up and inflate its abdomen to spew superhot lava from its body. Interrupt it during charge up to deal massive Stagger to it.


Enraged State: When enraged, the beast has increased ATK and uses its charge up skills more often. Applying Staggered to it ends the enraged state.
