Item description
Use this to designate and acquire one of the following 6★ operators: Ardelia / Pogranichnik / Last Rite / Ember / Lifeng.
A thick and heavy designation permit. Various operators have signed their names on it to provide you with their support.


Usage


This item can be used from the Valuable Stash menu to select and receive one of the following Employment Contracts, which is automatically converted to the respective Operator (or their Potential Token) and 
 
Bond Quota
.
