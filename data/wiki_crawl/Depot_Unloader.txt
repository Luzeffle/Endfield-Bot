Item description
In extreme environments, the Depot Loaders and Depot Unloaders can be directly connected to the facilities to ensure germ-free and low Blight transportation.


Usage


A placeable unloading point that transfers goods from a local depot into the AIC Factory. The Depot Unloader must be placed right next to the Depot Bus.
