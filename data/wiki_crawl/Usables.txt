Usables are the equivalent of consumables, which can be used to provide buffs to 
Operators
 or restore their HP.



Description


Usables are usually crafted using a combination of certain types of Items: 
Plants
 (or their Powders), 
Gatherables
 gained from defeating Enemies and Bottles or Jars produced by a 
Moulding Unit
.

Most recipes of Usables are locked until the obtaining and collecting of related Plants or Gatherables, which can be browsed via the 
Crafting Manual
, and some can be produced automatically from the 
AIC
.
