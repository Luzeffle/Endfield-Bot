Endfield Industries
, or simply 
Endfield
, is the protagonist faction of 
Arknights: Endfield
. The organization is led by the enigmatic 
Endministrator
, with its headquarters located aboard the orbital spacecraft 
OMV Dijiang
.

Established by 
Rhodes Island
 and her partners during the early settlement of 
Talos-II
, Endfield Industries was founded to advance the research, exploration, and industrial development of Talos-II. Endfield plays a pivotal role in addressing the challenges posed by Talos-II, including threats such as the 
Aggeloi
 and the 
Blight
, while pioneering technological innovations such as the 
Automated Industry Complex
 (AIC) and 
Protocol-Originium
 (PROTORIG).

Overview


Endfield Industries is an independent company that primarily focuses on providing pioneering industrial technologies and services. The Endfield vision is to build and expand into the frontiers of the Talos-II.

When the first pioneers arrived at Talos-II, Rhodes Island and her other partners jointly founded Endfield Industries to serve as a think tank and forward prospector. The Endministrator led numerous Endfield geological survey teams and generated detailed maps for numerous frontier regions. After the link between Talos-II and Terra was severed, the frontiersmates of the new world were forced to face the threats posed by the Aggeloi and the Blight on their own. Endfield thus stepped to the forefront, and continued to do so till this very day.


Endfield became an integral part of the future of Talos-ll due to its novel technologies such as Protocol-Originium teleportation, Automated Industry Complex (AIC), controlled Originium distribution, and OMV Dijiang (the only orbital vehicle of Talos-II). As dangers emerged to threaten the budding civilization of the new world, Endfield once again initiated her journey to expand the frontiers.
― Nexus files
