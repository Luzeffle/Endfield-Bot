Description


 
Laevatain, a prominent Sarkaz


The term "Sarkaz" does not actually refer to a specific race; rather, it is a derogatory umbrella term for various humanoid races who are neither 
Ancients
 nor 
Elders
. Originally being the natives of 
Terra
 calling themselves as "Teekaz," their dominance came to an end when the "
Predecessors
" from space colonized Terra and accidentally introduced both 
Originium
 and the Ancients and Elders upon the planet. As such, the Sarkaz were often persecuted and have engaged in millennia of conflict against the other races, though there were always those who wished for peace.

Sarkaz exhibit a wide range of physical traits depending on their tribes. The most commonly noticed traits are a pair of horns of varying shapes atop their heads, long, pointed ears, and tails of varying shapes such as having pointed ends or scales. However, there are some Sarkaz tribes lacking any of the aforementioned traits.
