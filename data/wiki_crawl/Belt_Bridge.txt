Item description
A logistics unit that AIC Factory managers can use to create an organized transport system and avoid the "spaghetti".


Usage


A logistics unit that lets 2 transport belts intersect without obstructing each other.

The Belt Bridge allows a transport belt to go over another without disrupting the flow of items on both belts.

Requirement :
