The 
Free City of Neo Tsukushi
 is a city affiliated with the 
Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
 located within the 
Civilization Band
 on 
Talos-II
. The city is home to the Neo Tsukushi Business School and serves as a business and commercial hub, hosting events and gatherings of business elites. 



Trivia


Neo Tsukushi is the Talos-II counterpart to 
Higashi
 on 
Terra
.
