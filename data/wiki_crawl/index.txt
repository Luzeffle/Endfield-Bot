 About 


Arknights: Endfield
 is a real-time 3D Role-Playing Game (RPG) with strategic elements developed by 
Hypergryph
 and published by 
Gryphline
. An initial technical test was held in mainland China (CN) on November 10, 2023, followed by Global (North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia) on January 12, 2024. A beta test for both CN and Global servers began on January 16, 2025. A second beta test for both CN and Global servers began on November 27, 2025, with the full worldwide release of the game on January 22, 2026.

 Featured Operator 


Laevatain
 is a 6 star 
Striker
 introduced with the launch of 
Arknights: Endfield
 and is the featured Operator from the 
Scars of the Forge
 banner in the Zeroth Directive version. 
Reconvener
 and Operator from 
Rhodes Island
, Laevatain joined 
Endfield Industries
 under the direct command of the 
Endministrator
.
When Laevatain's rapid Basic Attacks trigger a Final Strike, she can absorb the Heat Infliction from nearby enemies and convert them into stacks of Melting Flame. These stacks may also be gained by using her Battle Skill which summons a Magma Fragment to deal damage over time. When four stacks of Melting Flame is reached she can trigger Combustion by consuming all stacks, letting her perform an additional attack, as well as triggering her Combo Skill, that allows her to deal massive damage to all affected enemies. Additionally her Ultimate, Twilight, greatly enhances her Basic Attacks and Battle Skill!
Laevatain is voiced by Horie Yui in Japanese, Yan Meme in Chinese, Kum You-rim in Korean and Kira Buckland in English, the latter of whom is well known for voicing 2B from Nier: Automata.
