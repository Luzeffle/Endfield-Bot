Overview


The Operational Manual is a system that integrates various features, such as Activity Nodes, Daily Tasks, and Tactical Training. Use it to get familiar with various activities on Talos-II.
― Tutorial


This menu guides the player to various missions, challenges and tutorials that help progressing through the game.

Upon reaching 100 Activity Points and claiming the rewards one activated Vacation Tag will be automatically used to double the 
 
Pass Experience
 received. Other rewards are not affected by this.

Index


The 
Index
 menu provides quick access to various Sanity consuming activities. With the exception of Energy Alluvium, all other content takes place in instanced areas called 
Protocol Space
.



Protocol Spaces are scattered throughout Talos-II. These isolated spatial pockets can be explored by a competent team. Study the basic details, threat levels, and enemy intel of the Protocol Space before exploration. Some Protocol Spaces also offer salvageable resources. Explore them accordingly to gather the desired resources.
― Tutorial


These 
Protocol Spaces
 serve as the main source to obtain various progression materials for upgrading and promoting 
Operators
 or 
Weapons
. Completing a Protocol Spaces gives a fixed amount of rewards depending on the type and Level of the Rift cleared. Upon completing the Rift the player will be asked if they want to claim the rewards at the cost of 
 
Sanity
. Leaving or failing a Rift does not consume Sanity. Once the player has interacted with a Protocol Space in the world, they can enter the Protocol Space via the Index menu or by selecting the Protocol Space on the map without having to travel to the Protocol Space location in the world.

Some Protocol Spaces have special mechanics, such as continuous HP loss but HP restoration for killing enemies or shorter Combo Skill cooldowns. Players may want to adjust their squad to take advantage of the mechanics provided by the Protocol Space.

Energy Alluvium


Energy Alluvium may appear at certain locations of Talos-II. Approach an Energy Alluvium to activate it and view its details.
― Tutorial


Energy Alluvium
 can be found in set locations in the world of 
Arknights: Endfield
. Interacting with one gives the player the choice to start a challenge. A large amount of enemies will spawn in several waves, defeating all of them rewards the player with a first time clear reward. Enemies will also have a chance to drop their respective items and 
Essences
. The level of enemies spawned are affected by the Exploration Level.

After reaching 
Exploration Level
 4, all Energy Alluviums will turn into 
Severe Energy Alluvium
. On top of the items dropped by the enemies, the Severe Energy Alluvium will also give the option to obtain extra rewards (mainly 
 
Flawless Essence
) at a cost of 
 
Sanity
. It's also possible to use 
 
Valley Engraving Permit
 (or 
 
Wuling Engraving Permit
 in 
Wuling
) to guarantee certain stats on the Essences obtained.
