Northmarch Support Hub has, on multiple occasions, emphasized that their products were personal power tools designed for industrial and construction uses. This particular cutter proved mediocre for its intended purpose. However, it worked pretty well as a two-handed claymore.

Thermite Cutter
 description


Northmarch Support Hub
 is an arms manufacturer on 
Talos-II
. Although officially a manufacturer of industrial equipment and tools, it is in fact the leading suppliers of arms for the 
United Workers' Syndicates of Talos-II
.

Products


Weapons


Thermite Cutter
 - A Pioneer-series product from Northmarch Support Hub. The Mk IV thermal cutter was constructed using patented thermal materials and delivers powerful cutting performance. Positively reviewed by many UWST crewmates.


Former Finery
 - A high-end product of Northmarch Support Hub. This chain cutter was designed with older technology. Though mass production lines have been discontinued, the weapon still continues to prove its reliability in the field.


JET
 - An experimental weapon manufactured to the highest standards of Northmarch Support Hub. The prototype was designed by the now-disbanded Aerospace Research Center. An array of Originium compression jet engines gives this lance its turbo performance.


Rock Auger
 - A standard-issue Pioneer-series product from Northmarch Support Hub. This lance is constructed using treated alloys and features a demolishing drill tip with reliable, time-tested designs. The weapon is capable of handling common Aggeloid threats.


Wedge
 - A legendary work of Northmarch Support Hub that perfectly embodies the style and virtues of Northmarch weapons. Features devastating firepower, total reliability, and absolutely abysmal handling and recoil.


Detonation Unit
 - An Arts unit developed by Northmarch Support Hub. It is, without question, the unit with the best Originium Arts conductance. Only the most capable casters could master this weapon.
