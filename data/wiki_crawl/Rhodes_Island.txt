Rhodes Island Pharmaceuticals Inc.
 or simply 
Rhodes Island
 (
R.I.
) is a faction on 
Talos-II
. Originally a Terran organization founded as a pharmaceutical company to provide care and treatment to the Infected, Rhodes Island's operations on Talos-II were cut off from the main organization following the collapse of the 
Cosmic Gate
, leading to the establishment of an autonomous branch under Warfarin’s leadership.
