Refining Unit
 for further production.

Type of Refined Materials:

Ferrium

Grinding Unit

Packed Origocrust

Dense Origocrust Powder

 - with 
 
Cryston Powder


 
Steel
 - with

Powder


Certain ores and refined materials can be processed through 
 
Shredding Unit
 to become tiny powders, which is used in production of Batteries and for further processed materials.

Type of Powders:



 
Originium Powder


 
Origocrust Powder


 
Amethyst Powder


 
Ferrium Powder


 
Carbon Powder

Buckflower Powder


 
Citrome Powder


 
Aketine Powder


 
Sandleaf Powder


 
Jincao Powder


 
Yazhen Powder

Bottle


Identical to bottles in real world, Bottles are made from the 
 
Moulding Unit
 with refined materials, and their usage is usually as material for some Usables.



The fluid interaction button becomes available when you are close to a lake or reservoir. Use it to perform the 
Fill Fluid
 action. Fluid interaction is 
usable only in Jinlong region
 and not available in Valley IV.
― Tutorial


In 
Jinlong
, the Bottles can be used to fill fluid like 
 
Clean Water
, though to a limited capacity. This does not work in 
Valley IV
, however.

Type of Bottles:



 
Amethyst Bottle
 - 1 capacity for Fluid


 
Ferrium Bottle
 - 1 capacity for Fluid


 
Cryston Bottle
 - 1 capacity for Fluid


 
Steel Bottle
 - 1 capacity for Fluid


Item "
Steel Jar
" not found in database
  - 5 capacity for Fluid


Item "
Cuprium Jar
" not found in database
  - 5 capacity for Fluid


Part


Mechanical parts that are mandatory for facilities, Parts are made from the 
 
Fitting Unit
 with refined materials, and they're required to craft 
AIC
 Facilities.

Type of Parts:



 
Amethyst Part


 
Ferrium Part


 
Cryston Part


 
Steel Part


Item "
Cuprium Part
" not found in database


Component


Gear 
components
 are essential for gear assembly. You need different 
components
 for assembling pieces of gear of different quality.
― Tutorial


Circular discs that are identical to mechanical gears, Components are made from the 
 
Gearing Unit
 with refined materials, and are crucial for 
Gear
 assembly.

Type of Components:



 
Amethyst Component


 
Ferrium Component


 
Cryston Component


Item "
Cuprium Component
" not found in database


Solution


Fluid emitted from specific Plants, Solutions are made from the 
 
Reactor Crucible
 with powders of Plants, and is one of the core material for Usables.

Type of Solutions:



 
Jincao Solution


 
Yazhen Solution
