Dialogue


UWST scientists Linnaeus and Joan are trying to find a bottle of important medicinal sample that went missing. Linnaeus lost the bottle during the Landbreaker attack and it might be somewhere near the Transport Nexus or the work platform along the road. You decide to help them.






Linnaeus is out of research materials as the Landbreakers ate most of his medicine. He can only prepare another sample with a rare fungus. Joan recommends using her long-ears' tracking abilities to find the fungus. Though risky, the scientists agree to give it a try and ask if you can help track the long-ears.






Both trials are successful. Joan is overjoyed and really wants to feed the medicine to the long-ears. Linnaeus, on the other hand, sounds a little worried. From their conversation, you sense the two are more than just ordinary colleagues.
