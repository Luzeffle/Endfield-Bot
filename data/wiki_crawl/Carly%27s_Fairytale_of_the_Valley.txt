Carly from the Worker Dorms wants to draw a storybook that can make everyone happy. She hopes the Endmin can help her complete the book.
― Event description


Carly's Fairytale of the Valley
 is the first event in 
Arknights: Endfield
.



Participation Requirements


Complete main mission: 
Chapter I Process II: Paving the Way
.



Event Rules


During the event, visit various locales of 
Valley IV
 to start a camera challenge.


Refer to the goals of the photograph, add operators, adjust the formation, and include the target in the photo to complete the camera challenge.


During the event, 1 new camera challenge will be available every day for a total of 7 challenges. If the area of the camera challenge is still locked, unlock it by progressing through the main mission.


Complete camera challenges to earn generous rewards.
