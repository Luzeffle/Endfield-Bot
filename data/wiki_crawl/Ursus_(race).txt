Description


 
Da Pan, a notable Ursus


Ursi have the traits of 
bears
 (family 
Ursidae
), i.e., 
brown bears
, 
black bears
, 
polar bears
, and 
giant pandas
. Their ursine traits can be seen from their rounded ears, short and unnoticeable tails, and a bulky stature than other Terrans. The Ursi's most distinguishable feats are their tall height and outstanding physical strength compared to other races, which are attributes they generally like to emphasize. They also possess a highly-developed endurance as a result of the harsh environment they live in, i.e., frigid winters in the northern regions. The Ursi have a large number of subraces, each with different hair and fur color, though the most common ones are black, brown, and white.
