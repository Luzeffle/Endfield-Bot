Usage


An adjustable pipe logistics unit that controls the type and quantity of fluids passed through the pipe.

Pipe Control Ports can limit the fluid and the quantity of fluids allowed to pass through pipelines. Use Pipe Control Ports in blueprints to mark the type of fluid to be moved through various pipelines.
