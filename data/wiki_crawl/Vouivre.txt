Description


 
Yvonne, a notable Vouivre


Vouivres have the traits of "lesser 
dragons
;" creatures in various mythologies that more-or-less resemble dragons such as 
wyverns
, 
wyrms
, 
hydrae
, 
drakes
, and 
amphipteres
. They are almost identical to the 
Dracos
 such as unique-shaped horns, large dragon-like tails, and supreme physical prowess, and this causes others to mistake both races frequently given the scarcity of the Draco population. Their only difference from the Dracos are 
Originium Arts
 talents; Vouivres lack the innate talent to enhance superior Arts, especially pyro Arts.
[
1
]
 Vouivres are able to regulate their own metabolism to reduce food consumption that is most likely as a survival mechanism against the harsh deserts where they originate from.

Owing to their long-standing military tradition as royal guards, Vouivres are famous as excellent soldiers. Traditional Vouivre families will have their children receive stern military training as cultivation for new guards. Those qualified Vouivres will be dispatched to palaces to serve as royal guards. Others who are disqualified will simply search for alternative routes to continue their military career; as such, they become proficient soldiers and mercenaries. However, not all Vouivres follow this stereotype and many have sought out other occupations.

Etymology


Their name is an alternative name of 
guivre
, a dragon-like creature in traditional French folklore from which the wyvern is based on. The name itself derives from the Latin word 
vīpera
 (as in the English word 
viper
).
