Enemy description
A Landbreaker combatant equipped with heavy explosive gauntlets. These serve as the mainstay of Bonekrusher mobs. A Siegeknuckles's gauntlets serve as portable weapons for taking down fortified positions.

Abilities








A relentless enemy. Even after being interrupted, he is likely going to try attacking again.


This enemy uses his armored sleeve to scatter reactive armor scales. Keep a safe distance, especially when accidentally stepping on it.


This enemy will try to retrieve armor scales from the ground to get back to form, and will launch a powerful attack upon successful retrieval.
