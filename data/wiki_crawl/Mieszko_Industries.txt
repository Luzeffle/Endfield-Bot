Mieszko Industries
 is an arms manufacturer on 
Talos-II
. Originally a branch of the 
Mieszko Group
, a 
Kazimierzian
 corporate conglomerate on Terra, Mieszko Industries has established itself as a leading supplier of weapons and gear for the 
Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
.

Products


Weapons


Torrent
 - A high-end sword released by Mieszko Industries. The finely sculpted and engraved blade also offered reliable performance. This weapon was well-received by TGCC elites and upper society.


Grand Finale
 - A mid-tier product of Mieszko Industries. The sword features chic, avant garde designs in addition to an amazing price tag for its performance. When released, the weapon became one of the best-sellers of the year in the TGCC.


Exemplar
 - The latest standalone product from Mieszko Industries. Designs were based on a relic unearthed by the 
Cabal of Tranquility
. The weapon itself was furnished with highly classified technologies, allowing it to cut through almost anything with ease.


Finishing Call
 - A special Mieszko Industries weapon with a limited production run. This was the final design of the collaborative project pursued by the arms company and the Knights Major expert Cromwell.


Valiant
 - A mid-tier product of Mieszko Industries. The lance is rare yet highly sought after, thanks to its vintage designs that resemble ballista bolt as well as its effective field performance.


Sculptor of Time
 - A classic example of the Mieszko Future Vintage-series experimental weapons. Despite its innocuous appearance, this pistol was Mieszko's unique interpretation of the Golden Age of Terra.


Howling Guard
 - A mid-tier product of Mieszko Industries. This pistol is a perfect combination of vintage Lateran designs with modern industrial construction. A blue metallic coating gave the finishing touches for this unique work of art.


Chivalric Virtues
 - An Arts unit developed by Mieszko Industries. It has a special D64 steel chassis and is furnished with a suite of customizable modules and parts. The appearance of the weapon reminded people of the Arena fireworks show of the Major.
