Background


Andre grew up in a small and modest village on 
Talos-II
.
[
2
]
 His parents aspired for him to leave the village, attend a prestigious school, and seek a better future in 
Talos City
, the largest city in the 
Civilization Band
. Andre eventually secures a position as an engineer in the Engineering Division (EF-ED) of Endfield Industries, where his skills and expertise quickly proved invaluable.
