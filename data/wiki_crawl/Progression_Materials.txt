Progression materials are items that are used to level up 
Operators
 and 
Weapons
. They can be used to ugprade Operator skills and promote them, as well as tune Weapons.

Operator EXP


Operator EXP comes from two sources: 
Compact Scripts
, and 
Protologs
.



Compact Scripts can only be used for Operators with Level below 60.


Protologs can only be used for Operators with Level 60 or above.


Both Scripts and Protologs can be obtained from various ways, mainly the 
Rift: Combat EXP
.



Operator Upgrades


There are two types of Operator Upgrades: Promotions and Skill Upgrades.



Promotions use 
 
Protodisk
 (for E1/E2) or 
 
Protoset
 (for E3/E4).


Skill Upgrades use 
 
Protoprism
 (for Skill Level below 7) or 
 
Protohedron
 (for Skill Level 7 or above).


Protodisk and Protoset can be obtained from 
Rift: Promotions
, while Protoprism and Protohedron can be obtained from 
Rift: Skill Up
.



Weapon EXP


Weapon EXP comes from 
 
Arms Inspector
, 
 
Arms INSP Kit
 and 
 
Arms INSP Set
, which can be obtained from various ways, mainly through 
Rift: Weapon Up
.



Weapon Upgrades


Unlike Operators, Weapons have only one Upgrade: 
Tuning
, which can be done with 
 
Cast Die
 (for T1/T2) or 
 
Heavy Cast Die
 (for T3/T4).
Both can be obtained from 
Rift: Weapon Tune
.
