, or

 for short, influences the damage taken. All Operators have 0 and all enemies have 100 initial Defense, independent of their level. Defense is mainly increased by equipping Gear, and is otherwise only rarely increased by other sources. 
Catcher's

Equipping four pieces of Gear of the highest rarity gives 140 DEF, resulting in a 0.416 multiplier for incoming Damage, or 58.33% DMG Reduction. Enemies having 100 DEF results in a 0.5 multiplier.

This multiplier describes the effect gained from Agility and Intellect, as well as the elemental resistances of enemies. In-game these resistance values are shown as percentage points for Operators and rated with letters for enemies. Starting with 'D' for no resistance, or a 1.0 multiplier, 'C' for some resistance and so on. The accurate multipliers can be seen on the respective enemy pages. 
Corrosion
 takes effect in this multiplier.
