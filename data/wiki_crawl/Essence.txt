Acquisition


Defeated Enemies encountered in the world may drop Essences in different rarities, depending on the level of the enemy. While low level enemies drop Stable and Clean Essences, higher level enemies drop Pure Essences. Flawless Essences are only obtained by spending 
 
Sanity
 to claim rewards after clearing a 
Severe Energy Alluvium
, unlocked after reaching 
Exploration Level
 4.



Overview


Each Essence carries up to three 
Essence Stats
. Infusing a Weapon with a Essence will increase the level of the Weapon Skills that match the ones from the Essence. Higher rarity Essences have more and also higher leveled stats than lower rarity ones, while Flawless Essences can be further improved in 
Essence Etching
. Before infusion, players can preview the effects the Essence will grant.
An Essence can only be attached to one Weapon at a time. However, it can be unequipped and freely transferred to another Weapon at any point.



Stable Essences have only one Essence Stat, granting +1 level


Clean Essences have two Essence Stats, granting up to +2 levels


Pure Essences have three Essence Stats, granting up to +3 levels


Flawless Essences have three Essence Stats, granting +6 levels for Attribute and Secondary Stats and up to +3 for named Skill Stats


A maximum of 2000 Essences can be stored in the player's inventory. Unused Stable, Clean and Pure Essences can be disassemble for 20, 50 and 100 Stock Bills of the region they were acquired from respectively. Flawless Essences cannot be disassemble and are used in Essence Etching instead.
Equipping a Weapon with an Essence that matches all three Weapon Skills also changes its appearance, by adding a small animated glowing part.



Essence Etching


Flawless Essences can be upgraded through a process called etching, which is accessed via the Etch Essence menu. Etching allows the player to enhance a Flawless Essence by consuming another Flawless Essence.

During an etching attempt, one stat on the target Essence is selected by the player, along with an additional Flawless Essence to be consumed. If the attempt is successful, the selected stat is upgraded. If the attempt fails, the target Essence remains unchanged and the player instead receives 
10 
 
Coolant Gel
.

Coolant Gel can be used to guarantee a successful etching upgrade, upgrading the selected stat without consuming an additional Flawless Essence. The chance of a successful etching attempt decreases as the selected stat’s skill level increases, and is indicated using the ratings Very High, High, Low, and Very Low and is also reflected in the 
 
Coolant Gel
 costs required for a guarantee upgrade. The tables below list the success rates and 
 
Coolant Gel
 costs for the different upgrades.

Trivia


Turbid Essences are not obtainable in-game, but can be seen on a tutorial screenshot and as a filter option.


In Beta Test I there was no way to remove Flawless Essences, at that time called 
Flawless Sample
, from the players inventory, meaning the player could theoretically fill their inventory without having a way to make room for other Essences. But with the limited time of the Beta Test, it was not possible to reach the inventory limit.
