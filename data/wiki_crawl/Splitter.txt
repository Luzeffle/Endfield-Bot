Item description
A transport belt adjustment unit with a smart sorting module. Can be used to reduce the complexity of various facility supply lines.


Usage


A logistics unit that evenly divides product flow from 1 transport belt into multiple belts (up to 3).

The Splitter can split the product flow of 1 transport belt to up to 3 transport belts.

Requirement : 
Basic AIC Plan - Basic AIC II - Belt Splitting
