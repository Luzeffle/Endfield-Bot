The 
Free City of Kjersch
 is a city affiliated with the 
Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
 located within the 
Civilization Band
 on 
Talos-II
. The city is famous for its network of cable tramways that connect its mountain ridges, offering expansive views of vast snowfields.
