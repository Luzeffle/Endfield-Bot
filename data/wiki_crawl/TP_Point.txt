Gathering/Mining Site


You can gather all sorts of resources from 
Talos-II
. Some can be gathered immediately, while others must be 
attacked
 and smashed into smaller pieces before they can be gathered.
― Tutorial


A crucial part of the progression of 
Operators
, Gathering Sites contain Vitros and Fungis mandatory for Promotions and Skill Upgrades while Mining Sites contain Minerals that are mandatory for Tuning. Minerals are attackable and scatter after multiple hits to be collectable, while Vitros and Fungis can be collected from the get-go.

TP Point


Activate the 
TP Point
 to use its functions. The 
Rest
 function can resuscitate all disconnected operators and restore the entire team to 
Max HP
. When you are near a 
TP Point
, you can select the 
Manage Depot
 function and store Backpack items you do not need in the Depot to free up more Backpack space.
― Tutorial


Tele-Protocol Points, or TP Points for short, are scattered around the world. Once activated, the player can teleport to the TP Point by selecting it on the map and also gains access to various functions while in immediate vicinity:



Rest, to resuscitate disconnected Operators and restore the entire teams HP to max


Manage Depot, to move items between the Backpack and the local Depot


Submit  
Item "
Aurylene
" not found in database


Adjust Area Status, to modify the Area Status of the current Area


When a certain amount of  
Item "
Aurylene
" not found in database
  are submitted, various rewards will be given, mainly 
 
Origeometry
 and 
 
Stamina Delimiter
.

Electric Nexus


Electric Nexuses
 are usually used to power gates and switches. Hence, supply power to the Electric Nexus to control gates or switches linked to it.
― Tutorial


Electric Nexus generally act as an energy supply for switches to disable lasers or opening doors in the overworld; However, they are completely lacking in electricity, hence it's required to use 
Relay Towers
 to direct electricity to empower them for potential rewards beyond it.



Recycling Station


The Recycling Station will regularly fill up with resources. Approach a full Recycling Station to raise its material cache. Interact with it to collect all recycled resources.
― Tutorial


Recycling Stations recycle potential wastes in the overworld, and reproduce them as materials that are necessary for 
Dijiang
's development.

The Stations produce mainly Aerospace Materials, a mandatory material for upgrading facilities on Dijiang. They have a cooldown on collection, though.

VR Simulator


Many 
VR simulators
 can be found scattered across Talos-II. Approach and interact with these simulators to earn 
AIC Index
 and activate the specified 
simulation
.
― Tutorial


Devices resembling advertising balloons or tube men, VR Simulator are scattered around the lands of 
Valley IV
 and 
Jinlong
, and is a crucial part of AIC development.

The locations of VR simulators have been marked on the map. Use map markers to find these VR simulators. 
Simulations
 are used to simulate different scenarios for using the AIC. Once activated, the simulation can be directly accessed via the 
Operational Manual
 or 
AIC Factory Plan
. Use the simulations to master the proper use of AIC facilities.
