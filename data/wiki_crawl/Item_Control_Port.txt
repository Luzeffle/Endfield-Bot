Item description

Usage


An adjustable belt logistics unit that controls the type and quantity of items passed through the belt.

Item Control Ports limit the type and quantity of items allowed to pass through transport belts. Use Item Control Ports in blueprints to mark the type of items to be moved onto various transport belt sections.
