Dialogue


Rosa is extremely worried. Her lover, Romano, is trapped in Landbreaker territory. She asks if you could go there and find her love.








You meet Romano and he gives you another story. Rosa isn't his lover and she secretly planted a tracker in his watch. The man refuses to head back and asks you to return the watch to Rosa.








You return the watch to Rosa. She intends to keep looking for her love and bring him home.
