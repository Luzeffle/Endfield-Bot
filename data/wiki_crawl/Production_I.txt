Production I facilities are used to process raw materials into more practical items.

All Production I facilities require input material, and will give out output product or item.

Type of Production I facilities:



 
Refining Unit
 - produce Refined Materials from raw materials or ground powders.


 
Shredding Unit
 - produce Powders from raw materials.


 
Fitting Unit
 - produce Parts from Refined Materials.


 
Moulding Unit
 - produce Bottles from Refined Materials.


 
Planting Unit
 - produce Plants from Seeds.


 
Seed-Picking Unit
 - produce Seeds from Plants.
