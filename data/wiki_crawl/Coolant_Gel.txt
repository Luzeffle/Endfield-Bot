Item description
A special coolant gel used in the essence etching process. Results of etching are extremely sensitive to temperature changes. This process thus requires the use of coolant gels, which can be extremely expensive to make.


Usage


Coolant Gel can be used to guarantee successful essence etches. They are especially useful for the higher tiers of etching since those have extremely low chances of success, so gathering Coolant Gel can act as a form of guarantee, while lower tiers can be etched quite easily by just using essences themselves.
