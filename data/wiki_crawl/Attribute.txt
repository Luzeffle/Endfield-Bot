In 
Arknights: Endfield
, every 
Operator
 has four Attributes: 
Strength (STR)
, 
Agility (AGI)
, 
Intellect (INT)
, and 
Will
. These Attributes define an Operator’s performance by providing various bonuses. Each Operator has one of these four set as their 
Main Attribute
 and another as their 
Secondary Attribute
. Increasing the Main Attribute grants a significant bonus to Attack, while raising the Secondary Attribute provides a smaller Attack bonus. There is usually a strong correlation between an Operator’s 
Class
 and their Main and Secondary Attributes.

Attributes can also take decimal values, though only whole numbers are shown in the Overview. The full values are still used in calculations and are only rounded down for display purposes. The main way to increase Attributes is by equipping 
Gear
 and raising the Operator’s Level. In addition, some battle skills scale directly with certain Attributes, making them an interesting option for certain Operators.

Note that treatment received scales the base treatment amount, before any additional Treatment Bonus multipliers. For example, if character A has 1000 Will (corresponding to a 100% Treatment Received Bonus), character B has a skill that heals character A for 100 HP, and character B has an additional treatment bonus of 20%, character A will get healed for 220 HP, NOT 240. The additional healing from Will only scales the base healing.


Each point of Physical Resistance and Arts Resistance gives 1% DMG Reduction against the respective damage type, up to a maximum of 90% DMG Reduction. For example, 250 Agility translate to 20% Physical DMG Reduction.

Main Stats


HP


HP
, 
Max HP
, 
Health
 or 
Health Points
 represent how much damage an Operator can take. It is visible on the Operator screen and on the blue HP bar. If an Operator reaches 0 HP they will become disconnected and won't be able to participate in combat anymore. A disconnected Operator can be resuscitated or revived using consumable items like 
 
Arts Tube
 or by resting at a 
TP Point
.

All Operators have 500 Base HP at level 1 which linearly increases to 6000 Base HP at level 99. Additionaly each point of Strength increases the Base HP by 5. An Operator's Max HP can be further increased by certain Gear and Weapon effects. All sources of percentage increases to Max HP stack additively.
Some effects scale with the Max HP of an Operator. For example 
Ember's
 Ultimate and the ability of 
 
Thunderberge
.
