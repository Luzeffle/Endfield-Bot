Resourcing facilites can be used to collect precious resources like 
Naturals
, which can then be processed to create other products.

Mining Rigs are facilities specialized for mining. They can only be placed on mining spots in Mineral Beds. Different Mining Rigs can mine different minerals. Lower tier Mining Rigs are incapable of mining higher tier minerals. Each of the Mining Rigs will mine up minerals at a fixed rate depending on the purity of the Mineral Beds, with low purity granting 10/min and high purity granting 20/min. While Electric Mining Rig Mk II can mine all of the currently available Mineral Ores, it is not recommended to do so because despite requiring double the power of Electric Mining Rig, it does not generate more mineral.

Type of Mining Rigs:



 
Portable Originium Rig
 - Can only mine 
 
Originium Ore


 
Electric Mining Rig
 - Can only mine 
 
Originium Ore
 and 
 
Amethyst Ore


 
Electric Mining Rig Mk II
