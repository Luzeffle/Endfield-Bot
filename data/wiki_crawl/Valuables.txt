Valuables are special items that can be used to promote 
Operators
, upgrade their skills, and tune 
Weapons
. 



Fungi


Fungi-type items, generally found in Rare Gathering Sites, are necessary for the Promotion of Operators. They can also be reproduced in 
Growth Chamber
 with their Mycelia.



Vitro


Vitro-type items, generally found in Rare Gathering Sites, are necessary for the upgrades of skills. They can also be reproduced in Growth Chamber with their Tuber.



Mineral


Mineral-type items, generally found in Rare Mining Sites, are necessary for Tuning of Weapons. They can also be reproduced in Growth Chamber with their Seed (not to be confused with the Plant's Seeds).
