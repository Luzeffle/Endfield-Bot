Gear
 is one of the core equipment types in 
Arknights: Endfield
, along with 
Weapons
.



Overview


Each 
Operator
 can equip four pieces of Gear. One piece of Armor, one pair of Gloves, and two Kit pieces. These Gear pieces grant the Operator Defense as well as other stats such as 
Attributes
 and Secondary Stats.

Gear can be crafted in the Gear Assembly menu, using various Component items and Stock Bills. Most recipes are obtained as a reward from 
Authority Levels
. Recipes for higher level Gear are unlocked via Gear Template Crates.

Artificing


Artificing
, or 
Gear Artificing
, is a process used to increase the base stats of gold-quality gear by a fixed and predetermined amount. It is unlocked through the 
Gear Artificing
 mission in 
Wuling City
. Players can access this feature through the "Artificing" tab in the Gear Assembly menu. Each attempt at Artificing has a random chance to fail or succeed.

An artificing attempt costs one gold-quality gear and one Gear Artificing Catalyst of the matching region. A region's matching Gear Artificing Catalyst may be bought through its stock redistribution. Currently, only 
Wuling Artificing Catalyst
 are available. Each stat on a gear piece may be successfully improved up to 3 times, meaning any gear can be successfully artificed up to 9 times overall.

To begin artificing, the player must choose a gear and the stat they wish to upgrade, then choose a gear of a matching type (armor, gloves, or kit) to use as a material. The gear chosen cannot have a lower base value than the base value of the to-be-upgraded stat. Gear with a higher base value is labelled as a "Good Match" on the selection screen and has a higher chance of success than "Standard Match" gear. Materials will still be consumed upon attempt failure, but each failure adds to the individual gear's "Artificing Progress." Upon reaching the progress limit, the next artificing attempt will be guaranteed to succeed. The exact numbers for the progress limit are dependent on the gear and gear type, and are displayed on the artificing screen.

Currently, it is not always possible to get a "Good Match." For armor and gloves, it is only possible to get a "Good Match" for the second attribute stat by using a gear piece that has that attribute as its main attribute. Some kits only have one attribute stat, which has a higher base value than other kits with two attribute stats. For this reason, the non-set kits of the "Redeemer Gear Pack" group are an ideal artificing material as they can result in a "Good Match" for a main attribute, but that also means that these kits have no gear pieces that are a "Good Match" for themselves.

A gear's Defense cannot be upgraded.
