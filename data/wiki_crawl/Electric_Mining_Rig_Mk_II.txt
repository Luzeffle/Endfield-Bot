Item description
The Mk II upgrade of the electric mining rig features improved drill bit hardness, improved performance in rugged environment, and deeper mining capabilities.


Usage


Must be connected to the power grid with adequate power to work. This mining rig can mine Ferrium Ore and other minerals. Despite using more power, this mining rig does not produce resources any faster and thus the 
Electric Mining Rig
 should still be used for 
Originium Ore
 and 
Amethyst Ore
.
