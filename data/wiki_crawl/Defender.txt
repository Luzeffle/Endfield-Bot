classes
 or

Guard


Guards are skilled at making enemies 
Vulnerable
 and applying 
Physical Statuses
. They are also good damage dealers.
― Class overview












Guards mainly use either 
Swords
 or 
Polearms
 to deal 
Physical
 damage to enemies. They specialize in applying 
Physical Statuses
 and therefore also in dealing 
Stagger
 damage and typically include agility or strength as their main or sub attribute.



Caster


Casters are skilled at applying 
Arts Inflictions
 and 
Arts Reactions
. They are also good damage dealers.
― Class overview












Casters use various weapon types to deal immense Arts damage and can easily apply Arts inflictions to enemies. They typically have Intellect as their main attribute.

Defender


Defenders are extremely tough. They are skilled in protecting and treating their teammates, as well as counterattacking the enemy.
― Class overview












Defenders use Great Swords to deal 
Physical
 damage with Basic Attacks, but also have access to Arts damage via skills. They are also capable of treating their allies to restore HP. Defenders typically include strength as one of their preferred attributes.



Supporter


Supporters are skilled at applying various control effects that weaken the enemy. They can also buff and support their teammates.
― Class overview












Supports use 
Orbiters
 to deal Arts damage, helping in battle with various skills and talents that weaken the enemy and strengthen their allies. Will and intellect are usually the support's preferred attributes.



Trivia


Compared to the original game, 
Arknights
, 
Arknights: Endfield
 does not have the Sniper, Medic, and Specialist classes, which were removed in Beta Test II.


Notes




↑

Pathfinder's Beacon
, and does not appear in the original text. While the game files refer to them as "professions".
