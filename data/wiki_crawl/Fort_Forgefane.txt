Fort Forgefane
 is a fortress belonging to the 
Order of Steel Oath
. It is also an arms manufacturer and supplier that forges weaponry and armor for its knights.



Products


Weapons


The Fifth Heirloom
 - One of the weapons made by Fort Forgefane. Special smelting processes resulted in a wholly red blade. The Arts component utilizes a special liquefied Originium that resembles the first drop of blood shed by the Icefields.


Cerulean Resonance
 - One of the weapons made by Fort Forgefane. The grip was constructed using a special rod drawing process to enhance force damping and grip. The blade itself was forged with a rare glaciolithic mineral quarried from the wildlands.


Oathborn
 - Smelted and cast in Fort Forgefane, this is one of the most commonly used CQC weapons in the ongoing Aggeloi War. The great sword is incredibly easy to use. Even the most untested civilian can use it to defend themselves.


Aggeloslayer
 - A standard-issue lance made by Fort Forgefane to arm the common soldiery. Furnished with an extremely sharp tip specifically designed for fighting the Aggeloi.


Long Road
 - A standard-issue revolver made by Fort Forgefane to arm the common soldiery. This weapon was extremely popular due to its simplified artslock mechanism and incredible stopping power.
