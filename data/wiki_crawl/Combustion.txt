Effect


Friendly


Applying 
Combustion
 to an enemy deals 1 initial instance of 
Heat DMG
 and additionaly makes the enemy suffer 
Heat DMG
 as 
damage over time
. Forcibly applied Combustion does not deal the initial hit of 
Heat DMG
. If not otherwise specified, Combustion lasts for 10 seconds and will deal damage every second for a total of 10 hits. Triggering Combustion with a higher 
Status Level
 will increase the initial 
Heat DMG
 and the damage over time. The damage done also depends on the ATK, Arts Intensity, Heat DMG Bonus and level of the 
Operator
 that triggered the Arts Reaction.
