Profile


"Wind speed's okay. Visibility's good. Perfect weather for snowboarding."
Snowshine is a 
Rhodes Island
 operator assigned to 
Endfield Industries
 where she serves as a search and rescue (SAR) professional under the framework agreement between the two companies.
For well-known reasons, the true polar regions of 
Talos-II
 remain off-limits to humanity. However, as frontier expansion resumes, professionals like Snowshine who specialize in the geography of polar and high-altitude regions are in growing demand within the 
Civilization Band
.
Because of the significant talent gap, Snowshine and her rescue team are always faced with a busy schedule, even on days with no special missions.
"I like the snow, the white-capped mountains, and the shimmering beauty revealed only in the frigid cold... This is why I don't want them to leave painful memories to the people they touch."
