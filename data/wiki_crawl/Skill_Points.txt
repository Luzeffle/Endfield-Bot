Overview


Skill Points, or SP for short, are used to cast Battle Skills and are shared by all team members. Players can hold up to 300 SP, displayed in the HUD as three segmented bars of 100 SP each. A full bar is indicated with a pulsating yellow, while a partially filled bar appears white. Currently, all Battle Skills consume 100 SP when used.



 
Three SP bars on the HUD shown above the controlled Operator's health bar. A fully filled bar is indicated by the yellow color.


In Combat


During combat, SP regenerates naturally at a rate of 8 SP per second. Additionall SP can be gained through various actions:



Hitting an enemy with a 
Final Strike
 restores SP based on the controlled 
Operator
, ranging from 15 SP as Fluorite and up to 30 SP as Last Rite.


A Perfect Dodge recovers approximately 7.5 SP, but pauses the natural regenaration for half a second.


Performing a Finisher recovers SP based on the enemy.


Some Skills, notably those of 
Vanguards
, can also recover SP.


Many other Skills and effects can also 
return
 SP.


Returned SP is treated differently from naturally regenerated or recovered SP: it does 
not
 trigger SP Recovery-triggered effects and spending returned SP does not grant 
Ultimate Energy
.



 
The HUD at the moment SP is recovered by a Final Strike. The recovered SP is shown as a white flash, while the second bar briefly flashes yellow to indicate that it has been completely filled.


Outside of Combat


Outside of battle, SP rapidly regenerates to a maximum of 200, taking about 3 seconds from 0 to 200 SP, or drops at a rate of 40 SP per second when above 200.
When leaving combat, natural SP regenaration or loss is paused for around two seconds.
