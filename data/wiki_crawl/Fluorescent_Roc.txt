Item description
A weapon equipped by Endfield operators to significantly boost their combat capabilities.
One of the Arts units manufactured by Endfield Industries. It sports bold structural designs, superb Arts conductance, but is incredibly difficult to control. Mastering this weapon was considered a proof of competency amongst operators.
