Physical


Most Guard and Defenders 
Operators
 are capable of dealing Physical Damage, shown in game as a white number.













Some Operators are also capable of dealing 
Physical Statuses
, often requiring the enemy to be 
Vulnerable
:

Certain Skills also forcibly apply Physical Statuses without requiring enemies to be Vulnerable.



Vulnerable


Hitting an enemy for the first time with a skill with 
Physical Status
 applies 
Vulnerable
 instead of the actual Physical Status. A 
Vulnerable
 enemy can be 
Lifted
 and 
Knocked Down
 to add more 
Vulnerable
 stacks (up to 4). Hitting a 
Vulnerable
 enemy with 
Crush
 or 
Breach
 will consume the 
Vulnerable
 stacks.
― Tutorial


When an Operator uses a Skill that can inflict a Physical Status, but the target has no 
Vulnerable
 stacks, the Skill will instead inflict 
Vulnerable
 indicated by a broken shield besides their Health Bar. The effect lasts temporarily.

If the Skill is casted on a target with Vulnerable however, the Physical Status will be applied, causing the target to also take additional damage.

Do note that Elite and Boss enemies are immune to the control effect such as 
Lift
, but the Physical Status will still deal damage and Stagger as usual.

Heat


Heat 
Operators
 are capable of doing Heat Damage, shown in game as a red number.













Some Operators are also capable of applying 
Heat Infliction
, indicated by a fire icon besides the target's health bar.

When Operators apply 
Heat Infliction
 on enemies that are already have a Heat Infliction, a 
Heat Burst
 will be triggered, which deals some Heat DMG and adds one stack of 
Heat Infliction
. 



Combustion


Main article: 
Combustion


To an enemy with a non-Heat Arts Infliction, apply a Heat Infliction to consume both Arts Inflictions and trigger the 
Combustion
 reaction: 
making the target takes Heat DMG over time.
― Tutorial


If Heat Infliction is dealt on enemies with another type of Arts Infliction, 
Combustion
 will be triggered instead, during which they take Heat Damage and for a period of time, they also take continuous Heat Damage over time. If Combustion is dealt again, the duration will refresh.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.



Cryo


Cryo 
Operators
 are capable of doing Cryo Damage, shown in game as a cyan number.













Some Operators are also capable of applying 
Cryo Infliction
, indicated by an ice icon besides the target's health bar.

When Operators apply Cryo Infliction on enemies that already have Cryo Infliction, a 
Cryo Burst
 will be triggered, during which they take massive Cryo Damage.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.



Solidification


Main article: 
Solidification


To an enemy with a non-Cryo Arts Infliction, apply a Cryo Infliction to consume both Arts Inflictions and trigger the 
Solidification
 reaction: 
making the target temporarily unable to move.
― Tutorial


If Cryo Infliction is dealt on enemies with another type of Arts Infliction, 
Solidification
 will be triggered instead, during which they take Cryo Damage, and for a period of time, they are unable to act. If Solidification is dealt again, the duration will refresh. 
Elite
 and 
Boss
 enemies are immune to the effect, though.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.



Shatter


To an enemy with active Solidification, apply Vulnerable or Physical Status effects to trigger 
Shatter
: consuming the Solidification and 
dealing massive Physical DMG.
― Tutorial


If a Physical Status is dealt on enemies with Solidification, 
Shatter
 will be triggered, which removes Solidification, but dealing a massive amount of Physical Damage.



Nature


Nature 
Operators
 are capable of doing Nature Damage, shown in game as a green number.













Some Operators are also capable of applying 
Nature Infliction
, indicated by a leaf icon besides to the target's health bar.

When Operators apply Nature Infliction on enemies that already have Nature Infliction, a 
Nature Burst
 will be triggered, during which they take massive Nature Damage.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.



Corrosion


Main article: 
Corrosion


To an enemy with a non-Nature Arts Infliction, apply a Nature Infliction to consume both Arts Inflictions and trigger the 
Corrosion
 reaction: 
making the target gradually lose All Resistance over time.
― Tutorial


If Nature Infliction is dealt on enemies with another type of Arts Infliction, 
Corrosion
 will be triggered instead, during which they takes Nature Damage, and for a period of time, they also have gradually reduced Resistance. If Corrosion is dealt again, the duration will refresh, but the effect won't.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.



Electric


Electric 
Operators
 are capable of doing Electric Damage, shown in game as a yellow number.













Some Operators are also capable of applying 
Electric Infliction
, indicated by a lightning icon besides the target's health bar.

When Operators apply Electric Infliction on enemies that already have Electric Infliction, a 
Electric Burst
 will be triggered, during which they take massive Electric Damage.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.



Electrification


Main article: 
Electrification


To an enemy with a non-Electric Arts Infliction, apply a Electric Infliction to consume both Arts Inflictions and trigger the 
Electrification
 reaction: 
making the target temporarily take more Arts DMG.
― Tutorial


If Electric Infliction is dealt on enemies with another type of Arts Infliction, 
Electrification
 will be triggered instead, during which they take Electric Damage, and for a period of time, they take increased Arts Damage. If Electrification is dealt again, the duration will refresh.

The damage dealt this way is affected by the dealer's stats.
