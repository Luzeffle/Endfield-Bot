Assembly facilities are used to combine items into new ones.

All Assembly facilities require 
two
 input material, and will give out one output product.

Type of Assembly facilities:



 
Gearing Unit
 - produce Components from Refined Materials and Origocrusts.


 
Filling Unit
 - produce 
Usables
 from Bottles and Plant Powders or Solutions.


 
Packaging Unit
 - produce Batteries and 
 
Industrial Explosive
 from Parts and Originium Powders.


 
Grinding Unit
 - produce Ground Powders from Mineral Powders and 
 
Sandleaf Powder
.


 
Reactor Crucible
 - produce Solutions from 
 
Clean Water
 and Plant Powders.
