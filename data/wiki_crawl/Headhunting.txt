Headhunting
 is the method for players to obtain 
Operators
 in 
Arknights: Endfield
. It is the game's core "gacha" system.



Overview: Headhunting


Each headhunting pull costs 
500 
 
Oroberyl
, one 
 
Basic HH Permit
 (for Basic Headhunting) or one 
 
Chartered HH Permit
. Alternatively, the player can also perform ten consecutive pulls for 
5000 
 
Oroberyl
, ten 
 
Basic HH Permit
 (for Basic Headhunting) or 
10 
 
Chartered HH Permit
. There are functionally no differences between ten individual single pulls and a ten-pull, and the system does not differentiate or incentivize one or the other.

As is standard fare for most gacha games, and to limit the adverse effects of its gambling, headhunting features "fail-safe" mechanisms that are colloquially referred to as 
pity
:



A 5★ (at minimum) Operator is guaranteed to appear every ten pulls.

In other words, if a 6★ or 5★ Operator does not appear in nine pulls, then one will appear in the tenth pull. This means that at minimum, a 5★ Operator is guaranteed. This does not guarantee a 6★ Operator, however. A counter visible on the banner shows the remaining pulls until 5★ or higher Operator is guaranteed.


If a 6★ Operator does not appear after 65 pulls, each subsequent pull there after will receive a 5% rate up boost. As such, the 6★ Operator rate will be 5.8% at the 66th pull.

The pull count and increased rate (a.k.a. "pity") will be reset as soon as a 6★ Operator appears on any pull, and is carried over across different headhunting banners.


If the player doesn't get a 6★ Operator after 79 pull, the 80th pull will guarantee a 6★ operator. This does not guarantee a rate up Operator, however.


When obtaining a 6★ or 5★ Operator, there is a 50% chance to get the Rate up operator.


One time per banner, there's a guarantee system that if the player did not get the rate up 6★ Operator after 119 pull, the 120th pull is guaranteed to be the 6★ Operator on rate up. This does not carry over to other banners and will be reset when the banner ends.

Every 240th pull, a potential token of the 6★ Operator on rate up will be given. The number of pulls accounted will be reset when the banner ends.
