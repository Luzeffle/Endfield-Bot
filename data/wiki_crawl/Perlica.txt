Profile


Only by walking upon the land, can we truly appreciate every breath and pulse of life....And this is what Endfield is fighting for.
We once fought on and never gave up...for it is our wish to witness your return.


Perlica is the Supervisor of 
Endfield Industries
, a position she created. In this role, she oversees all company operations and serves as the official spokesperson. She is also a leading expert of Techno-Protocol and the development and application of 
Protocol-Originium
 technology, which makes her indispensible to the overall operations of the 
OMV Dijiang
. Her past contributions include spearheading key research that enabled the adaptive use of this technology and assisting in the refinement of the 
Automated Industry Complex (AIC)
.

Perlica is characterized by her calm and decisive leadership during crises. While the 
Endministrator
 was in hibernation, she assumed command of the 
Crisis Team
, leading its personnel against significant threats. She travels extensively throughout the 
Civilization Band
 to maintain order and ensure the operational continuity of Endfield Industries.
