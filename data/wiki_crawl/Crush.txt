Effect


Trying to apply 
Crush
 to an enemy adds 1 stack of 
Vulnerable
. If the enemy already has 
Vulnerable
 stacks, the actual Physical Status of Crush is triggered: All 
Vulnerable
 are 
consumed
 to deal 
Physical DMG
. The amount of damage done depends on the number of Vulnerable stacks consumed. The damage done also depends on the ATK, Arts Intensity and Physical DMG Bonus of the 
Operator
 that triggered the Physical Status.
