Arsenal Exchange
 is a method for players to obtain 
Weapons
 in 
Arknights: Endfield
. It is one of the game's core "gacha" systems.



Overview: Arsenal Exchange


In the 
Arsenal Exchange
 the player can use 
Arsenal Tickets
 (
 
) to exchange for supplies, mainly 
weapons
.

There are two ways to spend in Arsenal Exchange: Purchasing and Pulling.



Purchase Weapons


Some weapons are put on sale permanently or rotates regularly; the amount of Arsenal Tickets required correspond to the rarity:



4★ weapons cost 
20 


5★ weapons cost 
400 


6★ weapons cost 
2480 


Pulling Weapons


Weapons can be obtained through gacha. There are two types of banners known as 
Issues
, one being rotating with the Operator's banner, and one being permanent. Each issue attempt costs 
1980 
 and gives 10 random weapons from the list of available weapons, with the following rates:







Rarity



Percentage

Issuing features "fail-safe" mechanisms:



At least one 5★ or better weapon is guaranteed to appear in any of the issues.

In other words, if the first nine weapons are not 5★ or 6★ weapons, then the tenth weapon will be 5★ at minimum. This does not guarantee a 6★ Weapon, however.


If you don't get a 6★ weapon after 3 consecutive issues, the next issue will guarantee a 6★ weapon. This does not guarantee a Rate-UP weapon.


When obtaining a 6★ weapon, there is a 25% chance to get the Rate-UP Weapon.


One time per banner, there's a guarantee system that if you did not get the Rate-UP 6★ weapon after 7 consecutive issues, the Rate-UP 6★ weapon is guaranteed in the 8th issue. This does not carry over to other issue banners and will be reset when the issue ends.

At the 10th issue, one weapon selection box will be given. At the 18th issue, the Rate-UP 6★ weapon will be given instead. The cycle follows on every 8 issues afterwards. The number of issues accounted will be reset when the issue ends.
