Effect


Trying to apply 
Breach
 to an enemy adds 1 stack of 
Vulnerable
. If the enemy already has 
Vulnerable
 stacks, the actual Physical Status of Breach is triggered: All 
Vulnerable
 are 
consumed
 to deal 
Physical DMG
 and additionally makes the enemy take more Physical DMG for a certain duration. The inital amount of damage done, the duration and the increased damage taken depend on the number of Vulnerable stacks consumed. The inital damage done also depends on the ATK, Arts Intensity and Physical DMG Bonus of the 
Operator
 that triggered the Physical Status.
