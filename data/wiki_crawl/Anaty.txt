 (

race
 in the world of 
Arknights: Endfield
.




Description


 
Alesh, a notable Anaty


Anaties have rounded ears and long, pointed tails being either slim or rounded and bushy, though not all Anaties have bestial ears. They are a race that is commonly associated with the mountains because most of them have been living in such regions since the distant past. They also have the tradition of  welcoming any guest they could encounter. Despite their feeble bodies, Anaties are excellent diggers and mountain climbers thanks to their geographical adaptation. They can directly carve an entire cave system inside the mountains that serve as their dwellings.
