Description


 
Arclight, a notable Kuranta


Kuranta have the traits of 
equines
 (
Equus sp.
), i.e., 
horses
, 
donkeys
, and 
zebras
. Their equine traits can be noticed such as funnel-shaped ears, tall stature, and tails being either bushy being nicknamed the "skirt" or simply having tufts of long hair at the bottom of the tail dock. Although lacking the physical strength on par with the 
Ursi
, Kuranta possess an enduring stamina that grants them the ability to run at high speeds with little to no fatigue. The Kuranta's excellent stamina makes them talented soldiers, especially those who undergo adequate physical training and studies of military science.

Etymology


Their name might refer to the singular present active principle form of the Esperanto word for "to run," 
kuri
