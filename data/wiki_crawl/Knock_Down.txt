Effect


Trying to apply 
Knock Down
 to an enemy adds 1 stack of 
Vulnerable
. If the enemy already has 
Vulnerable
 stacks, the actual Physical Status of Knock Down is triggered: Adds 1 stack of 
Vulnerable
, deals 
Physical DMG
 and 10 
Stagger
. Weak enemies are also Knocked Down to the ground making them unable to act. Forcibly applied Knock Down does not need the enemy to be Vulnerable to apply its effect.

The amount of damage done has always a 120% DMG modifier, regardless of the number of Vulnerable stacks. The damage done also depends on the ATK, Arts Intensity and Physical DMG Bonus of the 
Operator
 that triggered the Physical Status. Arts Intensity can also increase the amount of 
Stagger
 dealt.
