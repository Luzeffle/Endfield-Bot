Description


 
Avywenna, a notable Cautus


Cautuses have the traits of 
leporids
 (family 
Leporidae
), i.e., 
rabbits
 and 
hares
. Their leporid traits can be noticed such as long rabbit ears, fluffy short tails, and average short stature. Their rabbit ears grant them a keen hearing sense which supplements their fragile body. Thanks to these traits along with their high fertility, Cautuses have a very large population. They treat their family members as an essential core; even when one decides to travel outside to settle down on their own, their family members will continually send their support be it material or emotional. Within the community, a family with the largest members are regarded as the core leader of the community.

Their name derives from the Latin word for "wary" or "cunning,"

 which is the origin of the English word 
cautious
