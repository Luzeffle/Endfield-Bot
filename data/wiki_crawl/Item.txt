Description


There are a large number of 
items
 in 
Arknights: Endfield
 with various uses depending on their type.



Items gathered from 
Talos-II
 or produced by the 
AIC Factory
 are either stored at the Backpack or Depot. Items cannot be placed in a full Backpack, but you can use the Manage Depot function at any 
TP Point
 or 
Core AIC Area
 to store them in the Depot.
― Tutorial


Crafting


You can access the [Crafting] screen from the Backpack panel. Crafting lets you make useful items from various raw materials. For example, crafting lets you use 
Buckflower
 to make 
Buckflower Powder
 that can provide HP Treatment.
― Tutorial


On the Backpack, you can do Crafting which allows you to craft items manually. This is mainly for crafting Usables or Plots.

A 
Crafting Manual
 is also there to guide on collectible items and recipes.
