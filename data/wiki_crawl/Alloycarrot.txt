The 
Free City of Alloycarrot
 is a city affiliated with the 
Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
 located within the 
Civilization Band
 on 
Talos-II
. Characterized by its extensive mining tunnels and subterranean infrastructure, Alloycarrot has a large Cautus population and is known for carrot cultivation and carrot-based cuisine.
